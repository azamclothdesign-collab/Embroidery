2) Business model (detailed)
We operate an online storefront that sells digitized embroidery design files as digital products only.

Customer journey:
1. Customer browses embroidery designs and categories on the website.
2. Customer adds one or more designs to the cart.
3. At checkout, the customer provides contact information (name, email, phone) and agrees to terms.
4. Customer pays online through the payment gateway.
5. On successful payment, the order is confirmed and embroidery ZIP packages are unlocked for immediate download.
6. No physical goods are manufactured, packed, or couriered. There is no COD settlement and no shipping logistics.

Revenue is earned only from successful online payments for digital downloads.

3) Exact use case for the intended PayFast payment gateway
We require PayFast as the checkout payment processor for digital product purchases on https://embdesigens.com.

Intended flow:
- Amount due = cart total for selected embroidery designs.
- Customer is redirected / processed through PayFast for payment (cards, Raast, bank account, and/or mobile wallets as enabled).
- On successful payment callback/confirmation from PayFast, our system marks the order paid and unlocks ZIP download access for that order.
- On failed or cancelled payment, no download is granted and no product is delivered.

This is a pure digital payment capture + confirmation use case. PayFast is not used for disbursements to end customers, marketplace splits, or physical shipment COD.
